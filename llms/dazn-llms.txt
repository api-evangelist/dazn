## APIs

DAZN publishes no public API. The following were probed on 2026-08-12 and all missed:

- No developer portal (`developer.dazn.com`, `developers.dazn.com`, `docs.dazn.com`, `api.dazn.com` do not resolve in DNS).
- No OpenAPI/Swagger at `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/v1/openapi.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/documentation`, `/documentation/json` or `/redoc` on `www.dazn.com`, `startup.core.indazn.com` or `api.playback.indazn.com`.
- No GraphQL endpoint, no AsyncAPI, no webhook catalog, no MCP server, no A2A agent card.
- The application backend hosts `startup.core.indazn.com` and `api.playback.indazn.com` are reachable anonymously but are undocumented internal service endpoints with no published contract, no terms of use for third parties, and no discovery surface. They are not a developer API and are not catalogued as one.
